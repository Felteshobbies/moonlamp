## What that means in practice

You may share and adapt these models, including printing them, as long as you

* **credit** the chain above and link to the license,
* state whether you changed anything,
* license your own derivatives under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** as well,
* and do **not** use them commercially. Selling prints of this lamp is not
  covered by this license.
