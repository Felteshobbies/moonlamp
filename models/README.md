# Models

**The printable files are not in this repository.** Together they are about
1.3 GB, and four of them exceed GitHub's hard 100 MB limit for a single file.
Git LFS would not help either: the free allowance is 1 GB of storage and 1 GB
of traffic per month, which a handful of clones would exhaust.

They are published on Printables instead, which is also where the license and
the remix chain live:

**[Illuminated Moon Wall Lamp — Remix](https://www.printables.com/model/1015789-illuminated-moon-wall-lamp-remix)**

## What you need to print

| Part | Notes |
|---|---|
| Moon relief, domed | Two halves for a 360 × 360 bed, or four quarters for smaller printers |
| Frame segments | Click together; a variant with fewer seams is included for the Prusa XL |
| Mounting brackets | |
| Back support | Optional |

The relief has a 60 × 60 × 3 mm pocket in the centre of its underside for the
electronics, with two 4 × 4 mm cable channels crossing it at 90° so the cable
can leave in any of four directions. The channels have 30° flanks and print
without support.

| File | Bounding box |
|---|---|
| Whole domed relief | 416.5 × 416.5 mm |
| Halves | 355.4 × 355.4 mm |
| Quarters | 294.3 × 208.2 mm |

## Printing notes

* The back stays flat on the bed — no supports needed anywhere.
* Drop the infill to **5–8 %**. The dome adds roughly 790 cm³ of enclosed
  volume that carries no load; at 15 % that is several hundred grams of PLA and
  many extra hours per part.
* The XY outline is unchanged from the original design, so the frame fits
  either relief, flat or domed.
* At a 20 mm dome height the summit sits practically flush with the 28 mm deep
  front edge of the frame.
